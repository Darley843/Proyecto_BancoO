Response
At Banco de Occidente, we integrate environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria across all 
aspects of our corporate strategy, with the aim of creating sustainable value and addressing major 
global challenges. This approach is reflected in the alignment of our operations with international 
initiatives, such as the Ten Principles of the United Nations Global Compact and the Sustainable 
Development Goals (SDGs), as well as in the adoption of reference frameworks that guide our 
management.
Our strategy is based on recognized national and international standards, which strengthen 
transparency, impact management, and accountability. Among the key frameworks and standards 
that guide our actions are the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), the Dow Jones Sustainability Index 
(DJSI), the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF), the UNEP FI Principles for 
Responsible Banking, Asobancaria’s Green Protocol, the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board 
(SASB), and the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD).
These commitments allow us to align our strategy with the sustainability expectations of our 
stakeholders and promote an organizational culture focused on managing positive impact. In this 
context, we have strengthened the development of sustainable financial solutions, the incorporation 
of ESG criteria into risk management—including the assessment of climate risks—and the 
measurement of financed emissions using methodologies such as PCAF.
We are also making progress in disclosing our performance under international standards such as 
GRI, SASB, DJSI, and TCFD, and in building internal capacity through sustainability training 
programs, thereby consolidating a management approach aligned with best practices and a long-term 
vision.
